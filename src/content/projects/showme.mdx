## <span id="overview">Overview</span>

ShowMe is a **client proofing and photographer portfolio** web app. This webapp aims to create a place for users to discover photographers, hire them, and receive their proofs in the same place.

Create an account, customize your profile, upload photos, create galleries, and hire photographers! Users can favorite and save photos. Discover new posts through the Explore page.
